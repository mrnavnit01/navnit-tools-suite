Frequently Asked
Is Navnit Tools really free to use?+
Yes — 100% free forever. No subscriptions, no ads, no hidden fees. Every tool is fully unlocked from day one.
How does the AI Background Remover work?+
It uses the remove.bg API — a state-of-the-art neural network trained to detect and remove backgrounds with pixel-perfect accuracy.
What file types does the PDF converter support?+
Any JPEG, PNG, or WebP image. We render a crisp A4 PDF with perfect aspect ratio fitting and clean margins.
How accurate is the KB Compressor?+
It uses a binary-search quality algorithm to converge within ±5% of your target. Far more accurate than simple quality sliders.
Is my data stored anywhere?+
No. All processing happens client-side in your browser. Images are only sent to remove.bg for background removal and immediately discarded after.
